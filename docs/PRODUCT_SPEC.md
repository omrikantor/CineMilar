# CineMilar - Product Spec

## The problem

People who love a specific movie or TV series often want more like it - but "more like it" is subjective. Generic "top rated" or "trending" lists don't capture *why* someone liked a specific title (the tone, the pacing, a character dynamic, a theme), so finding a genuinely similar next watch usually means manual research across reviews, forums, and recommendation threads.

## Who it's for

- **Users**: people looking for their next movie or TV series to watch, based on something they already enjoyed.
- **Customer**: same as the user - this is a direct-to-consumer tool, not something bought by an organization on someone else's behalf.

## Business goals

- Help users find a satisfying next watch faster than manual research.
- Build a base of recommendation data (what worked, what didn't) that improves over time as more people use it.
- Prove a simple, real value proposition: personalized, explained recommendations grounded in real data, not vague "you might also like" lists.

## Core capabilities

- Account creation and login, so recommendations and history are private per user.
- Enter a movie or TV series the user liked, plus free text describing *why* they liked it.
- Receive a ranked, explained shortlist of similar real titles (movies or TV series).
- Save and rate individual recommendations.
- View history of past searches and saved recommendations.
- (Stretch) Recommendations improve using feedback from other users who searched the same source title.

## Core user processes

1. **Sign up / log in**
2. **Request a recommendation** - pick a title (movie or TV series) and describe what you liked about it
3. **View ranked recommendations** - each with a poster, basic info, and an explanation of why it matches
4. **Save / rate a recommendation**
5. **View history** of past requests
6. **View saved / rated recommendations**
